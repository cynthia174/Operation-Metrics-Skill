# Evidence Policy

- Formal numbers come only from validated raw data or deterministic metrics.
- Formal judgments come only from official Rule Result; do not infer causes, categories, or health.
- Every conclusion must trace to Rule Result/evidence; otherwise say “数据不足/待确认”.
- Preserve source, dimension, period, and rule identity; do not mix CAC with CAC rate or repeat-lead rate with repurchase.
- Narrow conclusions when evidence is insufficient; never expand rule coverage.
