# Workflow: QA

QA is the `VALIDATING` phase of the full workflow:

1. Fact QA: numbers, periods, dimensions, rule states, and evidence are traceable.
2. Semantic Blocks QA: text, order, and semantic roles match Rule Result.
3. DOCX QA: pages, tables, pagination, readability, and styles.
4. OOXML QA: `document.xml`, `styles.xml`, `numbering.xml`, editable tables, and resources.
5. Record gaps, `NOT_EXECUTABLE`, and recovery actions.

Only the Definition of Done permits `DONE`; otherwise remain in `VALIDATING` or route to recovery. Unperformed visual QA must be reported as unperformed.
